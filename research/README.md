# BlueShare research

- `derivative-tracing/` preserves mathematical and freedom-of-dexterity work.
- `nlm-atlas/` preserves the NLM Atlas Schema over IP specification and PDF.
- `pruning/` contains the experimental pruning operator and its research

Research documents are retained as source material. Their presence does not
mean the described algorithms, hardware, safety systems, or protocols are
implemented by the current BlueShare runtime.
